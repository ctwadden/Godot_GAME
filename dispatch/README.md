# Dispatch

A classroom simulation of one fictional day on a gig platform, for IB Digital Society 4.2 (Employment and labour).

**Play:** https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/dispatch/

- Expert demo: https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/dispatch/?mode=demo
- Guided practice: https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/dispatch/?mode=student
- Driver round: https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/dispatch/?mode=driver
- Free play: https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/dispatch/?mode=free

Students change one platform rule at a time (commission, matching, rating cut-off, surge, Ontario's engaged-time minimum wage) on the same customers and explain who gains and who carries the risk. Guided practice asks for a prediction, checks it against the result, and ends with a question about a new case.

All values are fictional teaching assumptions except Ontario's general minimum wage ($17.95/h from 1 October 2026). Simplifications are shown in the game. No accounts, names, analytics or network calls. Saves stay in the browser and are not a submission; use **Report** to download your work.

Status: technically tested; not yet classroom piloted.

Watch first. Then try.

  1. Expert demo: press Next at your own pace. Follow a real order through payment, transport, stock receipt, automatic assembly and customer delivery. The expert then compares training, hiring, outsourcing, offshoring and reshoring. These are illustrations, not instructions to buy every option.
  2. Student practice: begin with $28,000, 80 processors, 10 screens and 80 batteries. Solve the screen shortage, choose one research approach and keep replenishing parts. Your target is 150 deliveries in ten days.
  3. You control: purchases, expertise and advancing time. The game handles automatically: assembly, delivery trucks and customer payments. There is no Deliver button.
  4. Ask for advice: the operations coach explains your actual stock and incoming shipments. A planned duplicate-scan fault gives you a chance to separate digital records from physical parts.
  5. Review your attempt: see your decisions and an illustrative alternative for the same number of days. Write your explanation and download the practice report. A missed target leads to review and retry; it is not a grade.

After practice, you can open the advanced 30-day campaign with the original budget, hiring systems and optional seeded disruptions. Demo, student practice and campaign keep separate progress.

Student practice and the advanced campaign have separate saves. Save keeps progress in this browser on this device. Clearing site data, using private browsing or changing computers can remove access to it. Download your report before leaving. No answers are uploaded to a teacher or server.

What you will practise

Separate who performs work from where it happens. Compare outsourcing, insourcing, offshoring and reshoring. Trace goods, data and money, diagnose a bottleneck, respond to disruption and justify a decision for different stakeholders.

Game profit is one outcome. Your explanation and ability to apply the ideas to a new case provide the learning evidence.

Open the project on a school computer
  1. Download the ZIP above and extract the entire folder.
  2. Open Godot 4.6.2 Standard, choose Import, and select the extracted project.godot.
  3. Wait for asset import, then press F5. No export templates are needed to run it inside Godot.

The ZIP contains the editable project, artwork, map data, tests, instructions and teaching guide. Godot itself is not included. If installing Godot is not available at school, use the browser link instead.

Browser support and troubleshooting

Use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge or Firefox browser with WebGL 2 enabled. If loading fails, check whether your school allows GitHub Pages and WebAssembly. A laptop screen is recommended; this prototype has not been redesigned for phone-sized screens. Browser downloads may need school permission.

All firms, prices, routes, lead times and event impacts are invented teaching examples. The basemap uses Natural Earth coastlines. No textbook pages or lesson slides are included.

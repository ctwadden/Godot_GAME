# Pick Rate

A classroom simulation of a fictional warehouse over a 10-day peak season, for IB Digital Society 4.2 (automation and employment).

**Play:** https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/pick-rate/

- Expert demo: https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/pick-rate/?mode=demo
- Guided practice: https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/pick-rate/?mode=student
- Free play: https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/pick-rate/?mode=free

Students set robots, a pick-rate target, breaks, a speed leaderboard and safety coaching, then see who pays: the company in costs, or workers in injuries, wellbeing and quits. A 10-season test separates a lucky season from the pattern. Guided practice asks for a prediction, checks it against the result, and ends with a question about a new case.

Every number is a fictional teaching assumption chosen to show relationships. None are real injury or turnover statistics. No accounts, names, analytics or network calls. Saves stay in the browser and are not a submission; use **Report** to download your work.

Status: technically tested; not yet classroom piloted.

PAYMENT & TOKEN LAB — GODOT

Extract the entire ZIP. Open Godot 4.6.2 Standard, choose Import and select project.godot. Wait for import, then press F5. Godot itself is not included; no extra plugins are needed.

Choose Demonstrate Mode A or B for a teacher-paced walkthrough, or select independent exploration. Controls never connect to a financial service. Everything is fictional.

Use Discussion for the static cost worksheet and historical e-trading source questions. Save/download the local evidence report before reloading or closing. Do not enter names or personal information.

Browser play: https://ctwadden.github.io/Godot_GAME/payment-token-lab/
